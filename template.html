About my work

Machine learning, from research to deployment.

I build learning systems that adapt to new conditions, reason from evidence, and run under real compute and memory constraints.

I’m a machine learning engineer at Milwaukee Tool and a final-year PhD candidate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with a concentration in Data Science. My industry work spans sensor pipelines, time-series learning, connected products, and embedded inference.

In research, I study continual and federated learning for limited-data adaptation, and LLM agents and evaluation for technical reasoning. My work connects algorithm design, rigorous evaluation, and working sensing systems.

I’m advised by Prof. Klara Nahrstedt and Prof. Chenhui Shao. Before UIUC, I earned my BS at Sharif University of Technology.
